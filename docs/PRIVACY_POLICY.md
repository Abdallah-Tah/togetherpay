# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Introduction

TogetherPay ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect the following personal information:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number
- **Payment Information:** Payment app handles (Venmo, Cash App usernames)
- **Group Information:** Group names, billing amounts, billing schedules
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Information:** App features used, timestamps, interaction data

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Log Data:** IP address, browser type, access times
- **Analytics:** App usage patterns, crash reports, performance metrics
- **Cookies:** Session cookies, preference cookies

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide Services:** Facilitate bill splitting and payment requests
- **Send Notifications:** Payment reminders, due date alerts, group updates
- **Improve Services:** Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, enhance features
- **Customer Support:** Respond to inquiries, resolve issues
- **Security:** Detect fraud, prevent abuse, ensure platform security
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with legal obligations, enforce terms

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

### Service Providers

- **Cloud Hosting:** AWS (database, file storage)
- **Email Services:** AWS SES (transactional emails)
- **SMS Services:** Twilio (payment reminders)
- **Push Notifications:** Firebase Cloud Messaging
- **Analytics:** Firebase Analytics, crash reporting services
- **Payment Processors:** Cash App, Venmo (for payment integration)

### Group Members

When you join a group, other group members can see:
- Your name
- Your phone number
- Your payment handle (Venmo/Cash App username)
- Your payment status (paid/pending)

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or to:
- Comply with legal process (subpoena, court order)
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect rights, property, or safety
- Investigate fraud or security issues

## Data Retention

We retain your information:

- **Active Accounts:** For as long as your account is active
- **Payment Records:** 90 days after payment completion
- **Inactive Accounts:** 1 year after last login, then deleted
- **Legal Requirements:** Longer if required by law

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

### GDPR Rights (EU Residents)

- **Access:** Request copy of your personal data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability:** Receive your data in machine-readable format
- **Object:** Object to processing of your data
- **Restrict:** Request restricted processing
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time

### CCPA Rights (California Residents)

- **Know:** What personal information we collect
- **Access:** Request copy of your data
- **Delete:** Request deletion of your data
- **Opt-Out:** Opt-out of sale of data (we don't sell data)
- **Non-Discrimination:** Equal service regardless of privacy choices

### Exercising Your Rights

Contact us at privacy@togetherpay.app to:
- Access your data
- Correct your data
- Delete your account
- Export your data
- Opt-out of communications

We will respond within 30 days.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption:** TLS 1.2+ for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest
- **Authentication:** Secure password hashing (bcrypt), optional 2FA
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access, principle of least privilege
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 security monitoring, intrusion detection
- **Audits:** Regular security audits, penetration testing
- **Backups:** Encrypted daily backups

However, no method is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy
