📸 How to Create Your Feature Graphic:

  1. Press F11 (or Cmd+Shift+F on Mac) to enter fullscreen mode
  2. Zoom to 100% (press Cmd/Ctrl + 0)
  3. Take a screenshot of ONLY the teal banner above (1024x500px area)
  4. On Mac: Use Cmd+Shift+4 then drag to select exactly the banner
  5. On Windows: Use Snipping Tool or Win+Shift+S
  6. Save as PNG and upload to Play Console Feature Graphic section

The banner above is exactly 1024x500px - perfect for Google Play Store!
