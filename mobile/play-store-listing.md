## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**Split Bills Together, Pay Separately**

TogetherPay makes managing shared expenses effortless. Perfect for roommates, families, group trips, and subscription sharing.

**Key Features:**

✅ **Easy Group Management**
• Create unlimited billing groups
• Invite members via email or link
• Track who's paid and who hasn't

✅ **Automated Payment Requests**
• Send personalized Venmo/Cash App payment links
• Automatic monthly or 4-week billing cycles
• Smart reminders for pending payments

✅ **Auto-Pay with Square** (Optional)
• Connect your Square merchant account
• Automatic Cash App Pay invoices
• Funds go directly to your account
• No pooled money, ever

✅ **Complete Transparency**
• Real-time payment tracking
• Member payment history
• Exportable receipts and reports
• Activity logs for all transactions

✅ **Secure & Private**
• Bank-level encryption
• No credit card storage
• OAuth-secured Square integration
• Your data stays private

**Perfect For:**
• Roommates splitting rent and utilities
• Families sharing subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, etc.)
• Friend groups managing shared expenses
• Small teams collecting dues
• Any recurring group payments

**How It Works:**

1. **Create a Group**
   Set up your billing group and invite members

2. **Set Shares**
   Define how much each member pays (equal or custom)

3. **Automate Collections**
   Choose manual links or automatic Square invoices

4. **Track & Confirm**
   See who's paid, send reminders, export reports

**Two Collection Modes:**

📱 **Manual Mode** (Free)
• Pre-filled Venmo/Cash App links
• Member marks paid, owner confirms
• Perfect for casual groups

💳 **Auto Mode** (Your Square Account)
• Automatic Cash App Pay subscriptions
• Instant payment confirmation
• Professional invoicing
• Great for regular teams

**Privacy & Security:**
• TogetherPay never touches your money
• Manual mode uses P2P apps directly
• Auto mode uses YOUR Square account
• All data encrypted in transit and at rest
• No third-party data sharing

**Why TogetherPay?**
Unlike other bill-splitting apps, TogetherPay doesn't pool funds or act as a payment processor. In manual mode, payments go directly between members via Venmo/Cash App. In auto mode, payments go to YOUR Square account. We simply automate the organization and tracking.

---

**Note:** TogetherPay is a payment request organizer, not a payment processor. We don't store card details or process payments directly. Square integration requires your own Square merchant account. Standard Square fees apply for Cash App Pay transactions.
